# Song Tracker

## Goal

Song Tracker enables users to search for the top 10 tracks of a musical artist and save their favorite tracks to a personalized list.

## Features

Search Functionality: Easily find musical artists by name.\
Top 10 Tracks Display: View the most popular tracks for the selected artist.\
Favorite Tracks List: Save your favorite tracks to a list stored in a database.\

## Target Users

Music Enthusiasts: Discover and keep track of popular tracks by top artists.\
Pop Culture Students: Research popular songs for academic purposes.\
Singer-Songwriters: Curate a collection of inspirational tracks.\
Social Media Users: Share favorite songs with friends on social platforms.\
Music Producers: Stay informed about trending tracks in the music industry.\

## Benefits

Convenient Discovery: Quickly find and save your favorite tracks.\
Easy Sharing: Effortlessly share music recommendations with friends.

## User Stories

### Music Lover and Listener
As a music lover, I want to search for my favorite musical artists to easily discover their top 10 tracks and find links to recordings I can listen to.

### Pop Culture Student
As a student in a pop culture class, I want to see a clear and visually appealing display of the top 10 tracks for the selected artist, so I can quickly identify popular songs relevant to my studies.

### Singer-Songwriter
As a singer-songwriter, I want to save my favorite tracks to a personalized list, so I can revisit them later and curate songs that inspire my work.

### Social Media User
As a social media user, I want to share my favorite tracks with friends on platforms like Facebook and Twitter, so I can easily recommend music and engage with others.

### Music Producer
As a music producer, I want a simple and efficient way to search for songs by my favorite artists and create a list of top tracks, so I can stay updated on what's trending.
